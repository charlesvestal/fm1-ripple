# Community patches

Pd patches by boochow (MIT, `LICENSE`), from his Heavy examples for Korg's logue SDK, the NTS-1 mk2 and the
drumlogue (github.com/boochow: loguesdk_hvcc_examples, nts1mkii_hvcc_examples, drumlogue_hvcc_examples), unchanged
but for their names:

- `logue-chiptune.pd`: `ChipTune.pd` from the logue SDK examples
- `logue-wavefolder.pd`: `WvFldr.pd` from the logue SDK examples
- `nts-vocoder.pd`: `7ch-Vocoder.pd` from the NTS-1 mk2 examples
- `nts-bitcrush.pd`: `BitCrush.pd` from the NTS-1 mk2 examples
- `nts-vca.pd`: `ChangeVol.pd` from the NTS-1 mk2 examples
- `nts-chorus.pd`: `StereoChorus.pd` from the NTS-1 mk2 examples
- `nts-reflection.pd`: `Reflection.pd` from the NTS-1 mk2 examples
- `nts-freqshift.pd`: `FreqShift.pd` from the NTS-1 mk2 examples
- `dl-monosynth.pd`: `MonoSynth.pd` from the drumlogue examples

The build makes them currents for the disk (`app/build/extra`, attached to each release). The droplets in
`examples/pd/` play them.
